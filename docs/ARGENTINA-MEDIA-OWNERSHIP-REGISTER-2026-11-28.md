## Executive findings

- **Grupo Clarín:** its own corporate-structure page names the Noble Herrera, Magnetto, Pagliaro and Aranda families as controlling shareholders and says 20% of share capital is listed. The page does not publish each family's individual percentage. [Corporate structure](https://ir.grupoclarin.com/estructura-societaria/).
- **América TV S.A.:** an Official Gazette notice records Daniel Vila as President, Claudio Belocopitt as Vice President and José Luis Manzano among directors following the 21 May 2026 shareholders' meeting. This is a governance record, not proof of equity percentages. [Official Gazette notice](https://otslist.boletinoficial.gob.ar/ots/download/589f5b391056fe98a649251a15e45bf2e38a5e267e6eb34b866885d3b146b38c/0/).
- **Grupo América:** Somos Pymes reported a 40% Vila-Manzano / 40% Claudio Belocopitt / 15% Eurnekian / 5% Nofal composition on 22 October 2026. This is a secondary report and remains unconfirmed against a primary shareholder register. [Report](https://www.somospymes.com.ar/item/20941-el-dueno-de-swiss-medical-adquirio-un-40-del-grupo-america.html).
- **Telefe:** Paramount's transaction counsel states that a sale to a holding company led by Gustavo Scaglione completed on 23 October 2025. A later La Política Online indexed report describes another Paramount sale in August 2026, but its indexed buyer details are inconsistent and the article body was inaccessible during review. Current ultimate ownership therefore remains unresolved in this edition. [2025 transaction notice](https://www.arnoldporter.com/en/perspectives/news/2025/11/arnold-porter-represents-paramount-in-sale-of-argentine-telefe) · [2026 disputed report](https://www.lapoliticaonline.com/politica/manzano-vila-scaglione-y-santa-maria-se-quedaron-con-telefe-4781/).
- **Blender and Carajo:** El Cronista and Letra E reported changes in Augusto Marini/CaleGroup's participation in May 2025. Their accounts differ on the transaction stage and the remaining interests of Iván Liska and Daniel Parisini. No primary cap table or signed transaction notice was reviewed; exact percentages remain unverified. [El Cronista](https://www.cronista.com/informacion-gral/augusto-marini-incrementa-su-participacion-accionaria-en-los-canales-blender-y-carajo/) · [Letra E](https://www.letrap.com.ar/letrae/economia-finanzas/el-negocio-del-streaming-la-argentina-javier-milei-crece-repite-esquemas-y-esperamonetizarse-n5416178/amp).
- **La Nación:** the Media Ownership Monitor page is explicitly a 2018 snapshot. It records S.A. La Nación as the mother company and lists 60% for the Saguier family and 10% for Bartolomé Mitre. These figures are historical only and are not represented as a current cap table. [Media Ownership Monitor 2018](https://argentina.mom-gmr.org/en/owners/companies/detail/company//grupo-la-nacion/).
- **Gelatina:** legal ownership was not established from a primary corporate filing in this edition. Ownership remains unresolved; presenters, guests, content or public associations are not substitutes for corporate evidence.

## Evidence classes

| Status | Meaning | Permitted presentation |
|---|---|---|
| `primary_source_confirmed` | Supported by a corporate disclosure, official filing or transaction-counsel notice | State only the exact claim and scope supported by the source |
| `reported_not_primary_confirmed` | Reported by a news publisher, without primary corporate confirmation | Attribute the claim to the publisher; never present it as verified ownership |
| `disputed` | Materially conflicting or inconsistent sources, with a written dispute basis | Display the conflict and withhold a current ownership conclusion |
| `historical_snapshot_only` | A dated historical research snapshot | Historical context only; never silently roll forward |

## Interpretation controls

1. Board membership is not shareholding.
2. A completed transaction at one date does not prove the present ultimate cap table after later transfers.
3. A media report is not a corporate register.
4. Historical ownership data must retain its original reference period.
5. No editorial stance or political affiliation is inferred from an owner, shareholder, presenter, guest, interview or programme.
6. Missing data is unknown, not zero and not proof of no ownership.
7. Percentages are not normalized or reconstructed from incomplete reporting.

## Coverage limitations and next research priorities

This first edition is a deliberately narrow sample. It does not yet provide a full corporate map of Grupo Indalo, Editorial Perfil, Grupo Octubre, Televisión Litoral, CaleGroup or every regional broadcaster. The next research pass should obtain current corporate filings, shareholder notices and legal-entity identifiers for those groups; verify the current Telefe cap table; and seek primary documentation for Blender, Carajo and Gelatina.

The registry is a research aid, not legal due diligence. It contains no live corporate-registry connection and no automated refresh. All claims require periodic source re-checking before commercial reuse.
